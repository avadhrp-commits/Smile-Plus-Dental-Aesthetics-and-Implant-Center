Hygiene & Technology

Sterilization that meets hospital standards.

Every instrument at Smile+ passes through a multi-stage sterilization cycle before it comes near you — because a luxury experience starts with an uncompromising one.

  • Autoclave sterilization for every reusable instrument
  • UV chamber & chemical disinfection cabinets
  • Glass bead sterilization for fine endodontic tools
  • Single-use disposable needles, gloves, masks & eyewear
  • Sealed, dated sterilization pouches — opened chairside per patient
  • Strictly monitored biomedical waste management
  • Portable X-ray & intraoral camera for precise, low-radiation diagnosis
