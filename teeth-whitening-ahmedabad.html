Why Choose In-Clinic Whitening?

Brighter, safely — not just brighter.

Professional teeth whitening uses a controlled-concentration bleaching agent applied directly to your teeth and activated in-clinic, lifting years of surface staining from coffee, tea, tobacco and everyday food far more effectively than any over-the-counter strip or kit.

The difference isn't just the result — it's the safety. A dentist checks your gums, existing fillings and enamel condition first, so the treatment is calibrated to your mouth specifically, rather than a one-size-fits-all home kit used without supervision.

Common Questions

Teeth whitening, honestly explained.

Does teeth whitening damage your enamel?

Dentist-supervised, in-clinic whitening uses controlled concentrations that are safe for enamel. The real risk comes from unsupervised home kits and overuse, which is why a professional assessment before whitening is recommended.

How long do teeth whitening results last?

Results typically last 6 months to 2 years depending on your diet and habits — coffee, tea, red wine and tobacco cause staining to return faster. Touch-up sessions can maintain your brightness over time.

Will teeth whitening work on crowns or veneers?

No — whitening only affects natural tooth enamel, not porcelain or composite restorations. If you have visible crowns or veneers, mention this at your consultation so shade matching can be planned properly.

Is there sensitivity after whitening?

Mild, temporary sensitivity to hot or cold is common for a day or two after treatment and settles on its own. Dentist-supervised whitening controls concentration and exposure time specifically to keep this manageable.
