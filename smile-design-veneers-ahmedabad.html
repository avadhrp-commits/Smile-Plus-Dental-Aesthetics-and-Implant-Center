What Is Smile Design?

Your smile, planned before it's touched.

Smile design is a digital-first process that maps your teeth's shade, shape, spacing and symmetry, then plans the exact changes needed to reach the smile you actually want — whether that's closing a gap, evening out chipped edges, or a full set of porcelain veneers.

Porcelain veneers themselves are ultra-thin shells bonded to the front surface of your teeth. They're custom-shaded to look like natural enamel, correct minor misalignment without braces, and resist staining far better than natural tooth surfaces.

Because Dr. Yashasvi Patel plans every case digitally first, you get to review and approve your new smile's shape before any preparation begins — no surprises at the final reveal.

Common Questions

Smile design, honestly explained.

Do veneers damage your natural teeth?

Modern porcelain veneers require only a thin layer of enamel to be reshaped — far less invasive than a crown. Done digitally and conservatively, your tooth structure underneath stays protected and healthy.

How long does a smile design treatment take?

A straightforward veneer case is typically completed in 2-3 visits over 1-2 weeks — digital planning and mock-up, preparation and impression, then final fitting. More complex full-smile makeovers may take a little longer.

Can I see my new smile before committing?

Yes. Smile+ plans every case digitally, so you can review a mock-up of your new smile's shape and shade before any tooth is touched.

How long do porcelain veneers last?

With good oral hygiene and avoiding habits like nail-biting or opening packets with your teeth, well-maintained porcelain veneers typically last 10-15 years or more.
